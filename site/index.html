§ 03  ·  Use cases

Reach for it when you need to look.

Small enough to leave open in a pane all day.

i.

Before you commit

Stage exactly what you mean to. Read your own diff side by side, then commit — with an optional AI draft of the message that you always review.

ii.

While an agent codes

Keep auri open in a split next to Codex or opencode (tmux, Zellij, your terminal’s panes). It refreshes on its own, so every change lands in front of you.

iii.

After a pull

Browse history, open any commit and see the diff of every file it touched — no git log incantations to remember.
